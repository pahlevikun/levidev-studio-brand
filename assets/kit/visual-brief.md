## Typography & balance

- **Primary wordmark (`Levidev`):** Outlined `Avenir-Roman` vector paths (zero external font dependency). Chosen so stem thickness (`~7.8px` at `76pt`) sits in optical balance with the `14.9px` rounded stone pillars (`rx=12`) of the icon—neither overpowering the mark (`Bold`) nor collapsing into hairlines (`Light`).
- **Secondary sub-label (`STUDIO`):** Outlined `Avenir-Roman` uppercase with wide tracking, paired with a `#98585C` rose accent bar.

## Three Logo Families (Light + Dark + Animated SVG)

1. **Icon Only (`icon-only`):** Original `levidev.studio/icon.svg` geometry (two stone vertical bars + two rose isometric strokes).
2. **Icon + Word Lockup (`icon + word`):** Square (`512×512`) and Horizontal (`520×240`) lockups.
3. **Full-Text Wordmark (`wordmark`):** Square (`512×512`) and Wide (`520×240`) typographic lockups led by the horizontal rose bar above `Levidev` and tracked `STUDIO` below.
